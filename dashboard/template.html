Resolved The United States Federal Government should reform its policy or policies for the regulation of non-agricultural wastewater discharge into navigable waters.
Discharge
“Any addition of any pollutant to navigable waters from any point source.” CWA §502(12)
Navigable waters
“The waters of the United States, including the territorial seas.” Scope set by the WOTUS rule and Sackett. §502(7)
Non-agricultural
The Act already excludes agricultural stormwater and irrigation return flows from “point source.” §502(14)
